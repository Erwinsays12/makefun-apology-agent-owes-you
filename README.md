Entry for the [make.fun competition "The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You"](https://make.fun/c/u-pucuys-the-one-sentence-apology-your-agent-owes-you-fc9786a0).

`main.ts` writes a single sentence of apology — an AI assistant owning up to
its user — to `/output/entry.txt`. The sentence was chosen by an internal
head-to-head tournament over a pool of 12 candidate apologies, judged by the
competition's own rubric: funniest and most human, one concrete scene plus
one sharp twist.

The winning apology:

> I'm sorry I proofread your wedding vows, approved them, and only afterwards
> noticed I'd autocorrected 'forever' to 'for now.'

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic, no network, no dependencies. Requires Node 22+.
